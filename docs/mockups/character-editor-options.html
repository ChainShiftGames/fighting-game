Three ways to rebuild the character editor

Every option is drawn with Testman's real data, and each one is shown on Standing Medium (5B). Each one explains a move the way you'd explain it to someone who has never played a fighting game: how you do it, how long it takes, where it hits, and what happens next. Drag the playhead in A and the slider in B. In C, the arrows show which moves can chain into which.

Whichever you pick

What all three share

  • Plain names first. Fighting-game terms sit behind a toggle for the two of us.
  • Frames come with time. “7 frames · 0.12 s”, plus a hint for what counts as fast or slow for that kind of move.
  • A verdict, not just numbers. On-hit and on-block advantage is worked out and said in a sentence.
  • Problems show up inline on the field that's wrong, not in a list at the bottom. Save stays blocked until the data is valid, same as now.
  • The 3D model is the preview, with hit areas drawn on it, scrubbed by frame number.
  • Its own look: custom theme, the game's Marcellus headings, custom-drawn timeline and canvas. No stock inspector widgets.
