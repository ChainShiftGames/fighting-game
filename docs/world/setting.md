Setting bible
Developed collaboratively; the creator has final authority. Anything not marked confirmed is exploratory until explicitly approved. Agents: don't add lore here unless asked; propose it instead.

Canon rule: Only explicitly approved details become established setting facts. Visual references guide art direction but do not automatically establish additional lore.

1. Core world concept
The world contains magical particles known as Aether.
Aether is a natural resource that exists throughout the world, including in the atmosphere and environment. Its density varies depending on factors such as location, seasons and weather.
Some regions have a high concentration of Aether, while others have very little.
These differences have influenced how civilizations develop, adapt to their environments and use available resources.
2. Magic and technology

The world has developed two broad approaches to dealing with Aether.

Aether-rich regions

Have developed ways to make use of the abundant magical particles in their surroundings.
Magic is a natural part of life, influencing how people interact with their environment and how their societies develop.

Aether-poor regions

Have had to find alternative ways to meet their needs.
These environments have encouraged mechanical and industrial technology, including dieselpunk-inspired machinery.

Technology and magic are not inherently opposed. They represent different approaches to living in a world with uneven access to Aether. Neither approach is inherently superior to the other.

3. Regions and geography

The final selection of regions, their geography, cultures, characteristics and relationships will be decided during the worldbuilding process.

Confirmed: The Glass Expanse
The name is approved.
Geography, culture, Aether concentration and other characteristics are not finalized.
Initial concept (open): desert landscapes and concentrated Aether deposits.
Confirmed: Aether-Rich Aristocratic Region

Core identity

The region has an exceptionally rich concentration of Aether, although its concentrations are not as extreme as those associated with the region containing major Aether deposits.
The region has a long history of producing powerful and renowned mages.
Its magical accomplishments are a significant source of regional pride.
The region's sense of pride has three interconnected foundations:
Natural advantage: The abundance of Aether makes magical practice more accessible and enables feats that may be considerably harder to achieve in other regions.
Historical achievement: Generations of powerful mages and notable magical accomplishments have established the region's reputation.
Institutional prestige: Accumulated knowledge, established magical traditions and prestigious education reinforce its standing as a center of magical excellence.
Some inhabitants regard their region's magical richness and accomplishments as evidence of their own superiority, leading to elitist attitudes toward outsiders.
Their historical achievements are real, but the belief that these achievements make them inherently superior is not universally shared.

Aether and the environment

Aether is abundant throughout the region, with some areas having substantially higher concentrations than others.
Aether is used to generate power and provide resources such as water and heat. These resources and conveniences can be expensive or difficult to obtain in other regions.
The abundance of Aether contributes to the region's prosperity and quality of life.
Particularly dense concentrations of Aether create an atmospheric pressure that ordinary humans struggle to withstand.
People exposed to these particularly dense areas can experience sickness and severe headaches.
Very powerful mages can withstand these conditions and live in the densest areas.
The exact nature of this pressure and its underlying mechanics remain open for further development.

Landscape and architecture

The region is characterized by rich blue-and-green plains and a lush natural environment.
Its architecture is predominantly white, with intricate stonework, ornate arches, elegant towers and refined detailing.
Turquoise and emerald-green roofs complement the white architecture.
Elevated bridges, suspended terraces and other magically supported architectural features are integrated into the landscape.
These structures create a sense of elevation and wonder without making the entire region a floating civilization in the sky.
Lush greenery is incorporated into architectural spaces, gardens and terraces.
Aurora-like skies and luminous Aether phenomena contribute to the region's distinctive appearance.
The precise geography, architectural techniques and behavior of these phenomena remain open for further development.

Fashion and cultural presentation

Fashion emphasizes intricate craftsmanship, elaborate details and refined presentation.
Accessories, ornate garments, jewelry and formal or royal attire are prominent parts of the region's visual identity.
Magic may be incorporated into clothing and accessories, although specific traditions and practices remain open.

Education and prestige

Magical education is highly respected.
Prestigious academies, accomplished tutors and established magical traditions contribute to the region's reputation.
Educational institutions may provide foundations for future regional factions, but those factions have not yet been established.

Society and wealth

The region has a strong aristocratic and high-class identity.
Its population includes wealthy commoners and non-mages who live there because of its status, prestige and social environment.
Wealth and social standing are not exclusive to magical ability.
The precise relationship between noble bloodlines, inherited privilege, wealth and demonstrated magical ability remains open for further development.

Political structure

The region is a monarchy, with royalty serving as the formal ruling authority.
The monarchy and its royal residence occupy a central position in the region. The exact location and structure of the royal seat remain undecided.
The most powerful mages inhabit or thrive in particularly dense Aether areas.
These individuals possess considerable influence because of their magical strength and exceptional reputations.
The monarchy remains the formal ruling authority, but certain individuals may rival the crown in practical power.
Rumors suggest that some individuals are powerful enough that even the monarchy might hesitate to oppose them.
The extent to which these rumors are true, the identities of these individuals and the precise limits of royal authority remain open.
No specific factions, political institutions or mechanisms of royal control have been established.
4. Regional politics and relationships

Political structures, trade networks and conflicts between regions will be developed after the regions are established. None are confirmed.

5. The global Aether crisis
Confirmed: The world's Aether circulation is becoming increasingly unpredictable. Concentrations no longer behave as reliably as they once did, creating the foundation for a global crisis.
Open: Its cause, severity, timeline and consequences.
6. Factions

Not yet established. They follow once regions, cultures and their relationships have taken shape. No factions from earlier brainstorming are confirmed.

The prestigious educational institutions and influential magical individuals of the Aether-Rich Aristocratic Region may provide foundations for future factions, but no specific factions are established.

7. Development principles

Current priorities, in order:

Establish the fundamental rules of Aether.
Establish the relationship between magic, technology and regional environments.
Develop and select the world's regions collaboratively.
Determine regional relationships and politics after the regions are established.
Develop factions after the regional foundations are in place.

Later: characters, the central storyline, tournament structure, detailed combat mechanics and other elements.

Goal: A coherent setting in which geography, Aether, technology and civilization fit together naturally while supporting the needs of a fighting game.

8. Visual style guide

This section establishes visual consistency for generated artwork. It describes the desired presentation of the setting, not additional in-world facts.

8.1 Global art direction
Favor polished fantasy concept art with clear silhouettes, strong environmental storytelling and readable architectural forms.
Artwork should communicate the world's identity through its environments, materials, clothing, technology and use of Aether.
Regional aesthetics should emerge from their environments and cultures rather than relying on generic fantasy imagery.
Maintain enough visual consistency for locations, characters, promotional artwork and fighting-game stages to feel part of the same world.
Exact rendering techniques, aspect ratios and levels of realism can vary according to the purpose of an image.
8.2 Aether-Rich Aristocratic Region: visual reference

Primary reference

The creator's approved reference depicts an elegant white architectural complex with turquoise-green roofs, intricate arches, elevated walkways, suspended terraces, lush greenery, clouds and a luminous turquoise Aether formation rising above the city.

This reference establishes the preferred balance between magical grandeur and grounded architecture.

Architecture

Predominantly white or ivory stone.
Intricate arches, columns, balustrades, carved details and elegant towers.
Turquoise and emerald-green roofs.
Elevated bridges and suspended architectural elements integrated into the surrounding landscape.
Gardens and greenery woven into the built environment.
Grand, refined architecture rather than uniformly imposing or militaristic structures.

Landscape

Rich blue-and-green plains, rolling green terrain and lush vegetation.
Architecture should feel connected to the landscape rather than isolated above it.
Elevated structures may create dramatic changes in height, but the region should not look like a civilization composed entirely of floating islands.
Clouds and mist may contribute atmosphere without obscuring the grounded setting.

Sky and Aether

Aurora-like atmospheric colors, especially turquoise, blue and green, with other hues remaining possible.
Luminous Aether formations can appear as dramatic environmental features.
Aether should feel integrated into the environment and architecture, not merely represented by generic glowing particles.
Avoid making every scene overwhelmingly bright or filling every available space with magical effects.

Fashion

Intricate fabrics, embroidery, decorative accessories and refined silhouettes.
Elegant formal wear, royal garments and elaborate jewelry.
Magical ornamentation may be used where appropriate.
Clothing should communicate refinement, wealth and prestige without requiring every inhabitant to dress like royalty.

Atmosphere

Beautiful, cultivated, refined and distinctly magical.
A sense of prosperity and elegance, supported by the environment itself.
The region may feel privileged or intimidating without defaulting to dark gothic imagery.
Its elitism should not make every scene hostile, sinister or oppressive.
8.3 Consistency checks for generated artwork

When generating artwork for this region, consider the following questions:

Does the image use the established white architecture and turquoise-green accents where appropriate?
Does the architecture feel elevated and fantastical without making the entire city airborne?
Is the landscape lush and connected to the built environment?
Do Aether effects feel integrated into the scene?
Does the artwork convey elegance, prestige and magical abundance?
Are any new details merely artistic interpretations, rather than accidentally treated as established lore?

Important: Individual images can explore different lighting, compositions, locations and architectural variations. The goal is recognizable regional identity, not identical scenery in every image.
