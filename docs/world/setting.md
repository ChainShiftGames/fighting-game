# Setting bible

> Developed collaboratively; the creator has final authority. Anything not marked confirmed is exploratory until
> explicitly approved. Agents: don't add lore here unless asked; propose it instead.

## 1. Core world concept

- The world contains magical particles known as **Aether**.
- Aether is a natural resource that exists throughout the world, including in the atmosphere and environment. Its
  density varies depending on factors such as location, seasons and weather.
- Some regions have a high concentration of Aether, while others have very little.
- These differences have influenced how civilizations develop, adapt to their environments and use available
  resources.

## 2. Magic and technology

The world has developed two broad approaches to dealing with Aether.

**Aether-rich regions**
- Have developed ways to make use of the abundant magical particles in their surroundings.
- Magic is a natural part of life, influencing how people interact with their environment and how their societies
  develop.

**Aether-poor regions**
- Have had to find alternative ways to meet their needs.
- These environments have encouraged mechanical and industrial technology, including dieselpunk-inspired machinery.

Technology and magic are not inherently opposed. They represent different approaches to living in a world with
uneven access to Aether. Neither approach is inherently superior to the other.

## 3. Regions and geography

The final selection of regions, their geography, cultures, characteristics and relationships will be decided
during the worldbuilding process.

### Confirmed: The Glass Expanse

- The name is approved.
- Geography, culture, Aether concentration and other characteristics are not finalized.
- Initial concept (open): desert landscapes and concentrated Aether deposits.

## 4. Regional politics and relationships

Political structures, trade networks and conflicts will be developed after the regions are established. None are
confirmed.

## 5. The global Aether crisis

- **Confirmed:** the world's Aether circulation is becoming increasingly unpredictable. Concentrations no longer
  behave as reliably as they once did, creating the foundation for a global crisis.
- **Open:** its cause, severity, timeline and consequences.

## 6. Factions

Not yet established. They follow once regions, cultures and their relationships have taken shape. No factions
from earlier brainstorming are confirmed.

## 7. Development principles

Current priorities, in order:

1. Establish the fundamental rules of Aether.
2. Establish the relationship between magic, technology and regional environments.
3. Develop and select the world's regions collaboratively.
4. Determine regional relationships and politics after the regions are established.
5. Develop factions after the regional foundations are in place.

Later: characters, the central storyline, tournament structure, detailed combat mechanics and other elements.

**Goal:** a coherent setting in which geography, Aether, technology and civilization fit together naturally while
supporting the needs of a fighting game.
